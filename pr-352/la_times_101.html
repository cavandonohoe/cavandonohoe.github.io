How I counted

I compared all 101 published entries in each edition with the complete history up to that year. An entry is a first appearance if it has no earlier match, a comeback if its latest match was more than a year earlier, and a previous edition pick if it appeared the year before.

I matched spelling changes, moves and relaunches of the same restaurant. A separately reviewed branch can debut on its own, as Badmaash Venice did in 2026. A grouped entry takes up one slot and can establish an appearance for its co-featured restaurants. For example, the 2025 Mercado La Paloma entry keeps Holbox and Komal in the running going into 2026. Mentions in broader market write-ups and the separate Hall of Fame are excluded. The 2020 edition used nine slots for profiles of people and organizations, shown as other honorees.

The Times’ 2018 roundup highlighted 31 debuts. This tracker counts 28 new slots that year. Bar Amá and Somni appeared in shared slots with previously listed restaurants, and Jon & Vinny’s had already appeared in 2015.

Sources are the L.A. Times archive of annual lists and the original edition linked for each year.
