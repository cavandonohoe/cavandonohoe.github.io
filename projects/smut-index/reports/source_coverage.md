# Source coverage and rights audit

Access date: October 31, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Exact URLs and source-specific
limitations are in `data/sources.json`. This report describes the corrected book-only scope.

| Source family | Coverage | Role | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Penguin Random House retail book page | GOT first-novel identity, publication date, edition | Primary bibliographic source | Edition page count is not narrative word count |
| George R. R. Martin author book page | First novel, excerpt link | Lawful acquisition lead | No complete downloadable novel obtained |

| LitCharts ACOTAR selected chapter guides | Chapters 2, 21, 27, 42, 46; full chapter-navigation inventory | Secondary event discovery | Same limitations; one summary can compress multiple acts |

The complete publicly navigable chapter inventory does not establish complete content
coverage. A successful webpage load does not imply that gated text was read. No paywall,
login, DRM or access control was bypassed. Empty guide responses encountered during
discovery were not interpreted as empty chapters. The committed source list contains the
pages actually used for this pilot, rather than a list pretending that every guide was reviewed.

Full novel PDFs appearing on unverified third-party sites were not downloaded or used.
Public accessibility alone does not establish authorization to redistribute a book.
No full novel, transcript, subtitle corpus, guide prose, or private source cache is committed.

Current annotations are original, short, abstract records, not replacements for the works.
Novel identities and chapter locations are attributed. The underlying sources retain their
rights; the project's license does not license them. Consult the register for provenance.

## What is required to complete the study

User-supplied lawful English-language copies or text exports of both first novels, with
edition/ISBN recorded. If the format is DRM-protected, obtain a lawful accessible format;
this project does not remove DRM. Keep source files private and share only derived abstract
annotations, numeric metadata, and aggregate figures. Complete scene segmentation and
independent annotation/validation are further requirements after text access.
